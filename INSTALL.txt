============================================================
 Look Mah, No Hands — install guide (macOS)
 Hands-free Ableton transport from facial gestures.
============================================================

WHAT'S IN THIS PACKAGE
  • LookMahNoHands-Detector.app  — the camera/gesture engine (no Python needed)
  • device/                      — the Max for Live device + its scripts
  • INSTALL.txt                  — this file

You need: Ableton Live with Max for Live (Max 9), and a webcam.


------------------------------------------------------------
 STEP 1 — Install the Max for Live device
------------------------------------------------------------
Keep the "device" folder together — the .amxd needs the .js files
next to it. Don't move just the .amxd out on its own.

  1. Copy the whole "device" folder somewhere you'll keep it
     (e.g. your Max for Live User Library, or just your Documents).
  2. In Ableton, drag "device/LookMahNoHands.amxd" onto a MIDI track.


------------------------------------------------------------
 STEP 2 — Open the detector app (one-time approval)
------------------------------------------------------------
The app is not signed with an Apple Developer ID, so on the FIRST
launch macOS will block it. This is expected. Approve it once:

  1. Double-click LookMahNoHands-Detector.app.
     macOS says it can't be opened / is from an unidentified developer.
     Click "Done" (do NOT move it to Trash).

  2. Open  System Settings → Privacy & Security.
     Scroll down to the Security section — you'll see a line about
     "LookMahNoHands-Detector was blocked". Click  "Open Anyway".

  3. Double-click the app again and click "Open Anyway" / "Open" to
     confirm. From now on it opens normally with a double-click.

  (On macOS Sequoia the old "right-click → Open" shortcut no longer
   works — you must use System Settings the first time, as above.)

  --- Faster alternative, if you're comfortable in Terminal ---
  Run this once and the app opens normally right away (no Settings trip):

      xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /path/to/LookMahNoHands-Detector.app

  (Drag the app onto the Terminal window to fill in the path.)


------------------------------------------------------------
 STEP 3 — Allow camera access
------------------------------------------------------------
The first time the app runs, macOS asks for camera permission.
Click "Allow". (If you miss it: System Settings → Privacy & Security
→ Camera → turn on LookMahNoHands-Detector.)

A preview window opens showing your face with a mesh + gesture meters.
That window is your "it's running" indicator.


------------------------------------------------------------
 STEP 4 — Use it in Ableton
------------------------------------------------------------
  1. With the detector running and the device loaded in Live, pick your
     camera from the dropdown ON THE DEVICE (in Ableton).
  2. Try the gestures:
        • Raise both eyebrows      → Play / Stop
        • Tilt head RIGHT          → Record-arm
        • Tilt head LEFT           → Undo
  3. To quit the detector: click the preview window and press  q
     (or just close the window).


------------------------------------------------------------
 TROUBLESHOOTING
------------------------------------------------------------
  • "App is damaged and can't be opened" → it picked up the download
    quarantine flag. Use the Terminal one-liner in Step 2.
  • No camera in the device dropdown → make sure the .app is running
    first, then click the dropdown's refresh.
  • Gestures don't fire / fire too easily → lighting and face vary per
    person; thresholds may need tuning (ask the person who sent this).
